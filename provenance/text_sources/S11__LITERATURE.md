# Literature scope and attribution

This pass is a mathematical continuation of the uploaded full-span audit. The new result is the all-common-noise entropy comparison and its geometric consequences. Background below was checked through primary sources on 6 September 2026. The search does not certify absolute novelty.

- V. Siddhu and R. B. Griffiths, *Positivity and nonadditivity of quantum capacities using generalized erasure channels*, arXiv:2003.00583. https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.00583 . The channel is their incomplete-erasure form with an auxiliary quantum-to-classical noisy measurement branch. Do not advertise a new channel class.
- F. Leditzky, D. Leung, and G. Smith, *Dephrasure channel and superadditivity of coherent information*, arXiv:1806.08327. https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.08327 . Repetition inputs, rate improvement, and positivity-threshold questions are established methods. Our full-span statement concerns a different auxiliary channel and must be positioned as a specific structural result.
- I. Devetak, *The private classical capacity and quantum capacity of a quantum channel*, arXiv:quant-ph/0304127. https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0304127 . This supplies the asymptotic meaning of positive block coherent information. It does not give an efficient decoder for these inner blocks.
- C. H. Bennett, D. P. DiVincenzo, and J. A. Smolin, *Capacities of Quantum Erasure Channels*, Phys. Rev. Lett. 78, 3217 (1997), arXiv:quant-ph/9701015. https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9701015 . The completely random-record endpoint is erasure up to independent flags.
- H. Barnum, J. A. Smolin, and B. M. Terhal, *The quantum capacity is properly defined without encodings*, Phys. Rev. A 58, 3496 (1998), arXiv:quant-ph/9711032. https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9711032 . Provides a capacity treatment including the erasure converse without the unproved assumption in the earlier presentation.
- C. Hirche and D. Reeb, *Bounds on Information Combining With Quantum Side Information*, arXiv:1706.09752. https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.09752 . Adjacent entropy-comparison literature; not used as a theorem substituting for the new certified inequality.

The targeted queries included the combination of noisy measurement, full span, coherent information, informational completeness, and incomplete erasure. They did not identify the precise all-common-noise theorem developed here. This is a search limitation, not proof that an equivalent theorem cannot exist under other terminology.

No application to noisy basis labels, heterogeneous errors, correlated records, or general fault-tolerant hardware is inferred from these references. The distinction between a construction-specific criterion and an exact all-code capacity theorem remains essential.
