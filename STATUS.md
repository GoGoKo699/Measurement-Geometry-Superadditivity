# Repository guide

This repository contains the precise channel model, geometric theorem, complete proof, computer-assisted certificate with three independent verification implementations, eight-use witness, and figures with their data and captions.

For every finite full-span measurement ensemble and every common symmetric reporting error strictly between zero and one half, the theorem gives a nonempty measurement-probability interval with globally optimized single-use coherent information equal to zero and positive unassisted asymptotic quantum capacity. [Exact theorem and assumptions](docs/MODEL_AND_CLAIMS.md#m04) · [Complete proof](docs/COMPLETE_PROOF.md)
