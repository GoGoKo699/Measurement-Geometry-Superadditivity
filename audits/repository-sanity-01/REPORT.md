## Independently established scientific account

The physical map is the stated flagged mixture of intact transmission and a classical reported-outcome measurement map. Summing hidden projective outcomes before conditioning on the receiver's record gives the effects in M01/P01. The receiver has no true-sign, error-bit or discarded-qubit register. The reference block has determinant `c t(1-t)/4`; classical flag entropies cancel. Every mixed qubit input is represented by its smaller eigenvalue `0<t<=1/2` and arbitrary Bloch direction. Pure inputs attain zero coherent information. Thus the infimum defining `Gamma` yields the exact globally optimized one-use threshold; a directional grid or nearly pure ansatz is not substituted for optimization.

The entropy integral in P03 follows by integrating the four logarithms of the two eigenvalues, zero and their sum. Its boundary terms cancel. The paired expression is concave in `g=c/(1-a x)` and convex/increasing in `x`; these are compatible changes of variable. The common reporting error gives a single chord coefficient across all axes. The polynomial comparison consequently implies `Gamma >= min(G_min,ell)` for every input, rather than assuming the generally false equality `Gamma=G_min`.

The analytical low-noise proof covers `0<c<=2^-28`, including arbitrarily nearly pure inputs. The high-noise proof covers `24/25<=c<1` through the entropy series and a controlled Taylor remainder. The compact proof uses exact joining endpoints and an analytic `0<t<=10^-6` tail; `10^-6` is not an excluded-input cutoff. Monotonicity in noise and concavity in input weight justify the entire-band lower cost, secants, and tangent or conjugate upper envelopes. The computer-assisted proof is therefore an enclosure over continuous domains, not interpolation between numerical samples. See [the entropy audit](entropy/REPORT.md).

The geometric inequalities are applied before directional minimization. Spherical averaging, the arcsin-series bound and the frame trace give the conservative gap `Gamma >= L+(3ca/35)lambda`. Positive weights and full span imply `lambda>0`. With fixed channel parameters, this gives the included lower and excluded upper endpoints of the nonempty interval stated in M04. No uniform gap or rate across degenerating geometries/errors is inferred.

The repetition argument includes the negative all-measured contribution. Central sign classes give lower bounds on nonnegative sums, without physical postselection; the polynomial denominator `(m+1)^J` is valid for every fixed finite ensemble. The exponential comparison selects a positive **finite** inner block before applying the standard quantum coding theorem to repeated uses of that block. It does not require a positive limit of the inner repetition rate as its length diverges. The coplanar identity, both reporting-noise endpoints and the cone formulas/expansion are correct in their stated domains. See [the coding and geometry audit](coding/REPORT.md).
